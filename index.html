Appliance Energy Use in Australia

A simple overview of how household appliances consume electricity in the Australian market, and how to read the clues that reveal what they really cost to run.

Quick facts

  • Energy Rating Labels (the star-rating stickers on TVs, fridges and washers) let you compare running costs between models at a glance — more stars means less electricity for the same job.
  • Standby power is real power. Devices left on standby (the little red light) can quietly add around 5–10% to a household's electricity bill over a year.
  • Heating and cooling are typically the biggest energy users in an Australian home, but entertainment equipment — especially televisions — runs for hours every day and adds up fast.
  • Choosing efficient models pays back. A more efficient appliance usually costs a little more up front but saves money on every electricity bill for the rest of its life.
